# REBECOMING — Lulu Paperback Setup

Print-on-demand via **Lulu.com**, sold direct from mkparrish.com so you keep the margin
(you pay print cost + a small Lulu fee; no Amazon cut). Steps below get you a buy link
to paste back into the shelf.

## 1. Create the book on Lulu
- lulu.com → Create → **Print Book**
- Category: Paperback / Perfect Bound
- **Trim size:** 6 × 9 in (US Trade)
- **Interior:** Black & white, **premium cream** uncoated paper (best for prose)
- **Cover finish:** Matte (recommended) or glossy

## 2. Interior file (the book block)
- Source: `public/downloads/ebooks/rebecoming.pdf` (currently **56 pages** at 6 × 9)
- Note: that PDF includes the screen-style cover panel as page 1 and 16 lined "working
  notes" pages at the end. For a clean paperback you want: title page → copyright page →
  body → (optional) blank. Ask me to generate a **print-clean interior** (proper front
  matter, no screen cover, notes pages removed/kept as you prefer) and I'll produce it.
- Perfect binding needs **32+ pages** — REBECOMING clears this easily.

## 3. Cover file (wraparound, one PDF)
At 56 pages on cream stock:
- **Spine width:** 56 × 0.0025 ≈ **0.14 in** (too thin for spine text — keep spine clean)
- **Full cover size (with 0.125 in bleed all around):**
  - Width = 0.125 + 6 + 0.14 + 6 + 0.125 = **12.39 in**
  - Height = 9 + 0.125 + 0.125 = **9.25 in**
- Front-cover art: `public/downloads/covers/rebecoming-cover.jpg` (1600 × 2560).
  Ask me and I'll build the full **wraparound cover PDF** (front + spine + back blurb) to spec.

## 4. ISBN
- Use a **free Lulu-assigned ISBN** (fine for selling direct), or your own if you have one.

## 5. Pricing
- Suggested retail: **$39** (set on the shelf; adjust anytime).
- Lulu shows your print cost at setup; your margin ≈ retail − print cost − small Lulu fee.

## 6. Finish
- Publish / set the book to **"Sell on your own"** (Lulu storefront link), copy the
  product URL, and send it to me. I'll drop it into the REBECOMING shelf entry and the
  "Paperback — $39" button goes live.
