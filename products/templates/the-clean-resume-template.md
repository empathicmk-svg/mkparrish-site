# The Clean Resume Template

### An ATS-safe, human-readable resume layout with prompts built into every section.

**Format:** Digital template (PDF + EPUB)
**Price:** $12
**Length:** One-page structure, fill-in prompts, examples, and formatting rules
**Audience:** Job seekers, career pivoters, senior operators, creatives, and anyone who needs a cleaner page fast

---

## Why this exists

The best resume design is quiet.

It does not hide the work behind columns, icons, tiny type, or decorative lines that confuse applicant tracking systems. It gives the reader the proof fast. A recruiter is not trying to admire the page. They are trying to decide whether to keep reading.

This template gives you a clean structure and the prompts to fill it well.

> Make the page easy to scan. Make the proof hard to dismiss.

Use the template first. Then use the examples to sharpen the language.

## Header Rules

Keep the header simple and searchable.

Use:

- Name
- City/state or "Remote"
- Email
- Phone
- LinkedIn
- Portfolio, if relevant

Skip:

- Full mailing address
- Icons
- Headshot
- Decorative monograms
- Multiple phone numbers
- Quotes in the header

Your name can be larger. Everything else should be boring on purpose.

## Summary Formula

Use one of these.

### Formula A - Career pivot

```
[ROLE / IDENTITY] with a background in [PAST FIELD] and strength in [TRANSFERABLE SKILL].
Known for [OUTCOME / EDGE]. Moving toward [TARGET ROLE] where [VALUE YOU BRING].
```

Example:

```
Operations-minded project manager with a background in hospitality and creative production.
Known for turning moving parts into calm systems people actually follow. Moving toward
client operations roles where speed, communication, and judgment matter.
```

### Formula B - Senior operator

```
[ROLE] with [YEARS]+ years in [CONTEXT]. Strongest in [PROBLEM TYPE],
with a record of [PROOF / OUTCOME].
```

Example:

```
Marketing operations lead with 8+ years in B2B SaaS and founder-led teams.
Strongest in messy handoffs, campaign systems, and reporting cleanup, with a record
of turning scattered processes into repeatable revenue support.
```

### Formula C - Creative or strategist

```
[ROLE] who helps [AUDIENCE / TEAM] [OUTCOME] through [METHOD / EDGE].
Experienced across [CONTEXTS] with strength in [SKILL].
```

Example:

```
Brand strategist who helps early-stage founders turn vague ideas into language
people can understand and buy. Experienced across websites, launches, and content,
with strength in voice, positioning, and proof.
```

## Bullet Rules

Good bullets have weight. They are not job descriptions copied from HR.

Use this pattern:

```
Strong verb + thing you owned + scope + result.
```

### Strong bullet examples

- Built a client onboarding tracker in Airtable that gave sales, delivery, and leadership one shared source of truth.
- Led weekly campaign operations across email, paid social, and sales enablement for a four-person marketing team.
- Rewrote internal reporting into a one-page dashboard used by leadership to spot blocked revenue and delayed launches.
- Coordinated 30+ vendor, client, and internal stakeholders across a six-week production timeline with no missed delivery dates.
- Simplified the intake process from six disconnected forms into one workflow, reducing duplicate requests and manual follow-up.

### Weak words to replace

Replace:

- Responsible for
- Helped with
- Assisted in
- Worked on
- Handled
- Involved in

With:

- Owned
- Built
- Led
- Supported
- Coordinated
- Improved
- Rebuilt
- Launched
- Managed
- Simplified
- Translated

If "assisted" is true, be specific about your piece of the work. Supporting work still has value when the scope is clear.

## One-Page Layout Rules

Use these settings unless your industry expects something else.

- Font: 10.5 to 11.5 pt for body text
- Margins: 0.5 to 0.75 inches
- Name: 18 to 24 pt
- Section headers: bold, all caps, simple
- Dates: right aligned or same line, but consistent
- Bullets: 2 to 5 per role
- File format: PDF for sending, DOCX only when requested

Avoid:

- Two-column layouts
- Text boxes
- Tables
- Icons
- Skill bars
- Photos
- Tiny gray type
- Decorative script fonts

Clean does not mean plain. Clean means the reader does not have to fight the page.

## The Ten-Second Scan

Before sending, give yourself ten seconds and answer:

- What role is this person aiming for?
- What are they clearly good at?
- What is the strongest proof on the page?
- Does the top third make me want to keep reading?
- Can I find their contact information immediately?

If you cannot answer those quickly, the page is not clean yet.
