## What This Is

AI can make content faster. It can also make you disappear inside your own feed.

The AI Content Twin is a practical voice system for creators who want AI help without losing the human signal: voice capture, point-of-view prompts, de-slop editing passes, content variants, and a simple guardrail document you can reuse every time you draft.

This is not a prompt dump. It is a way to train the machine on your taste, your boundaries, your rhythm, and your actual opinions.

> AI should not replace your voice. It should hold the rough draft while you bring the judgment.

## Section One: Build The Voice Snapshot

Before you ask AI to write, give it something real to imitate.

Fill this in:

- My voice is: [three adjectives]
- My voice is not: [three adjectives]
- I believe: [three strong points of view]
- I refuse to say: [banned words and claims]
- My audience comes to me for: [reason]
- A sentence that sounds exactly like me: "[sentence]"
- A sentence that sounds fake for me: "[sentence]"

### The Voice Snapshot Prompt

Paste this:

```text
You are helping me build a reusable voice guide. Analyze the writing samples below for rhythm, sentence length, emotional temperature, repeated vocabulary, point of view, and what the writer refuses to do. Do not write content yet. Return:
1. voice traits
2. sentence patterns
3. banned phrases
4. strongest beliefs
5. editing rules for future drafts

Samples:
[paste 5-10 posts, captions, emails, transcripts, or notes]
```

Keep the output as your base voice guide. Do not start over every time.

## Section Two: The Point-Of-View Prompt

Generic AI happens when you ask for content before you ask for judgment.

Use this:

```text
Act as my content strategist. Before writing, help me sharpen the point of view.

Topic: [topic]
Audience: [specific audience]
What most people say about this: [common belief]
What I actually believe: [your belief]
Offer or next step: [offer]

Give me:
1. the strongest tension
2. 5 sharper angles
3. 10 hooks
4. the one angle most likely to build trust
Do not draft the post yet.
```

This makes AI do strategy before production. That is the difference.

## Section Three: Draft In Your Voice

Once the angle is clear, use this:

```text
Using my voice guide below, draft a [format] for [platform].

Voice guide:
[paste voice guide]

Angle:
[chosen angle]

Rules:
- open with tension
- avoid generic motivational language
- use concrete examples
- keep the CTA natural
- do not use these words: [banned list]
- do not invent results, clients, numbers, or personal stories

Draft 3 versions:
1. direct
2. warmer
3. sharper
```

Never ask for one version. One version makes you accept the machine's first instinct. Three versions give you taste decisions.

## Section Four: The De-Slop Edit

AI has tells. Remove them.

Use this prompt:

```text
Edit this draft to remove generic AI language. Keep the meaning, but make it sound more human, specific, and voiced.

Kill:
- vague transformation language
- corporate filler
- repeated sentence rhythm
- overexplaining
- fake urgency
- phrases like game-changer, unlock, leverage, elevate, in today's fast-paced world

Keep:
- the point of view
- concrete examples
- emotional truth
- natural cadence

Return:
1. tightened draft
2. what you cut
3. where it still sounds generic
```

### The Human Pass

After AI edits, you do one final pass:

- Would I say this out loud?
- Is there one sentence only I would write?
- Is the claim true?
- Is the CTA proportional?
- Is there a real next step?

If the answer is no, it is not done.

## Section Five: Platform Variants

Use one idea in multiple rooms.

```text
Take the core idea below and translate it into platform-native versions without changing the point of view.

Core idea: [idea]
Voice guide: [voice guide]
Offer/CTA: [offer]

Create:
1. TikTok/Reels script under 45 seconds
2. LinkedIn post under 180 words
3. Instagram carousel outline, 7 slides
4. Story sequence, 5 frames
5. Email opening section

Each version should use a different doorway: story, checklist, mistake, example, or opinion.
```

This is how you get speed without content soup.

## Section Six: The Guardrail Doc

Keep this pinned:

**AI may:**

- draft hooks
- make variants
- organize raw notes
- turn transcripts into outlines
- pressure-test clarity
- create first-pass captions

**AI may not:**

- invent personal stories
- invent metrics
- pretend to have used a product
- flatten the opinion
- use claims I cannot defend
- publish without human edit

Creators do not lose trust because they used a tool. They lose trust when the tool makes a promise the creator would not stand behind.
