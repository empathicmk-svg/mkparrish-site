## Section One: What UGC Actually Sells

UGC sells one of five jobs:

1. **Attention:** the opening moment stops the scroll.
2. **Understanding:** the product becomes easier to understand.
3. **Trust:** the buyer hears a real person explain why it matters.
4. **Objection handling:** a fear or hesitation gets answered.
5. **Action:** the viewer knows what to do next.

Every concept you pitch should name the job. "I can make three videos" is weak. "I can make three objection-handling videos for first-time buyers" is stronger.

## Section Two: The Five Brief Types

### 1. The Problem-Aware Brief

Use when the buyer knows the pain but not the product.

Hook frames:

- "If you keep [problem], try this."
- "I did not realize [problem] was coming from [cause]."
- "This is the tiny thing that fixed [annoyance]."

Structure:

1. Name the problem.
2. Show the product in context.
3. Explain the mechanism.
4. End with the simple next action.

### 2. The Demo Brief

Use when the product needs to be seen.

Hook frames:

- "Watch what happens when..."
- "I tested [product] so you do not have to."
- "Here is exactly how I use [product]."

Structure:

1. Show the product immediately.
2. Do the action on camera.
3. Narrate the change.
4. Name who it is for.

### 3. The Objection Brief

Use when buyers hesitate.

Hook frames:

- "I thought this would be [objection]. I was wrong."
- "If you are worried about [objection], watch this."
- "The question I had before buying..."

Structure:

1. Admit the hesitation.
2. Test or explain the answer.
3. Show proof.
4. End with the buyer feeling safer.

### 4. The Comparison Brief

Use when the buyer is choosing between options.

Hook frames:

- "[Old way] vs [new way]."
- "I replaced [alternative] with [product]."
- "The difference between cheap and useful..."

Structure:

1. Show the before.
2. Show the product.
3. Compare one real decision point.
4. Do not exaggerate.

### 5. The Lifestyle Proof Brief

Use when the product is emotional, aesthetic, or identity-driven.

Hook frames:

- "The thing I did not know I needed..."
- "This made my morning feel less chaotic."
- "A small upgrade that actually stayed in my routine."

Structure:

1. Put the product inside a real moment.
2. Let the feeling be specific.
3. Show usage, not just possession.
4. Close with memory, not hype.

## Section Three: Script Templates

### Demo Script

"I wanted to test [product] because [problem]. Here is what I noticed first: [detail]. I used it for [specific use], and the biggest difference was [change]. If you are [buyer], this is useful because [reason]."

### Objection Script

"I almost skipped this because I thought [objection]. The part I did not understand was [mechanism]. After using it, [proof]. So if your question is [question], here is my honest answer: [answer]."

### Founder/Expert Script

"Most people think [common belief]. The real issue is [truth]. That is why [product/category] matters. When [buyer] uses it correctly, [result]."

### Short CTA Lines

- "Save this before you buy."
- "Use this if [specific condition]."
- "Start here if you are comparing options."
- "This is the feature I would check first."
- "If I were buying again, this is what I would want to know."

## Section Four: The Monthly UGC Retainer

Retainers are easier to sell when the scope is boring and useful.

Example monthly package:

- 8 raw videos
- 16 alternate hooks
- 1 monthly creative call
- usage rights for 30 days
- 1 revision round
- delivery in one organized folder

Do not promise virality. Promise a usable testing bank.

Position it like this:

"This gives your paid team enough raw creative to test angles every week without starting from zero."

That is a business offer. "I make cute videos" is not.

## Section Five: The Brand Intake

Ask every brand:

- Who is the buyer?
- What are they already trying?
- What objections stop them?
- What claims can I legally make?
- What claims should I avoid?
- What formats have worked before?
- Where will the content run?
- How long do you need usage rights?
- What is the approval process?

Good UGC does not start with filming. It starts with understanding what the asset has to do.
