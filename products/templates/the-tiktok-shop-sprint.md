## What This Is

Creator commerce works when trust survives the sale.

The TikTok Shop Sprint is a 14-day plan for testing affiliate products with taste: pick the right products, build a content angle, post with repetition, track what moves, and protect the voice that made people listen in the first place.

This is not a "post links everywhere" plan. That is how audiences burn out. This is a sprint for creators who want revenue without making every post feel like an ad.

> The goal is not to sell everything. The goal is to become trusted around a few things that make sense.

## Section One: The Product Fit Filter

Do not start with commission. Start with fit.

Score each product from 1 to 5:

| Filter | Question |
|---|---|
| Audience fit | Would my audience already care about this problem? |
| Voice fit | Can I talk about this without sounding fake? |
| Proof fit | Can I show or explain the product clearly? |
| Repeat fit | Can I make 5-10 angles without boring everyone? |
| Margin fit | Is the payout worth the attention? |

Only test products scoring 18 or higher.

### Avoid These

- products you would never mention without a commission
- products that require claims you cannot verify
- products that do not fit your audience's life
- products that need too much explaining for a short video
- products that make your feed feel cheaper

Money is useful. Audience trust is the asset that earns it again.

## Section Two: The Content Angles

Every product needs multiple angles. One post is not a test.

Use these seven:

1. **Problem:** "If you keep dealing with [problem]..."
2. **Demo:** "Watch how this works."
3. **Routine:** "Where it fits in my day."
4. **Objection:** "I thought this would be [concern]."
5. **Comparison:** "Before I used this, I did [old way]."
6. **Proof:** "Here is the detail that sold me."
7. **Gift/occasion:** "Who I would buy this for."

If you cannot create seven angles, the product may not be content-worthy.

### Hook Bank

- "I was influenced, but I checked the thing everyone skips."
- "This is the feature that actually matters."
- "I would not buy this for everyone. I would buy it for [specific person]."
- "Before you buy [category], look for this."
- "I tested [product] for [time]. Here is what held up."
- "The ad did not convince me. This did."
- "If your [problem] is not improving, try changing [specific behavior]."

## Section Three: The 14-Day Sprint

### Days 1-2: Pick And Prepare

- Choose 3 products max.
- Write your product fit scores.
- Gather proof: specs, claim limits, personal notes, screenshots, common questions.
- Write 7 angles per product.

### Days 3-5: Soft Launch

Post one useful piece per product. No hard selling yet. You are testing which problem the audience recognizes.

Track:

- watch time
- saves
- comments with questions
- profile taps
- link clicks

### Days 6-9: Double Down

Take the best angle and create variations:

- shorter hook
- more direct demo
- objection version
- story version

This is where creators quit too early. One post tells you almost nothing. Four variations tell you what the audience actually heard.

### Days 10-12: Convert

Now make the CTA clear:

- "I linked the exact one."
- "Start with this if you are comparing options."
- "Use the product page to check sizes/colors/details."
- "Save this before you decide."

Keep the content useful. The CTA can be direct without making the post desperate.

### Days 13-14: Review And Keep Or Cut

For each product, decide:

- Keep as recurring recommendation
- Keep for seasonal push only
- Cut from the rotation

The best affiliate system is not endless promotion. It is a small shelf of products you can stand behind repeatedly.

---

## Section Four: The Trust Rules

Use these rules every time:

- Disclose clearly.
- Do not claim results you cannot verify.
- Show flaws when they matter.
- Say who should not buy it.
- Keep non-sales content alive during the sprint.
- Stop promoting a product if the audience reports bad experiences.

Creators lose trust when every recommendation sounds equally urgent. Let some products be "not for you." Specificity sells better anyway.
