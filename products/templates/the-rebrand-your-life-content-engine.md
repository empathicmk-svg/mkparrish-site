## The Four Pillars

### 1. Rewrite Your Story

Personal reinvention, identity shifts, confidence, survival, ambition, dating, work, appearance, aging, family, grief, and becoming visible again.

**Best formats:** story posts, TikTok monologues, Substack essays, Patreon notes.

**Core line:** I am not starting over. I am editing.

### 2. Make the Message Clear

Website audits, positioning breakdowns, copywriting before and afters, service page fixes, LinkedIn profile rewrites, offer clarity.

**Best formats:** carousels, teardown videos, screen recordings, LinkedIn posts.

**Core line:** Pretty is nice. Clear gets paid.

### 3. Build the Thing

Digital products, ebooks, Stripe pages, Vercel builds, GitHub workflows, content systems, AI supported execution, creator operations.

**Best formats:** behind the scenes, tutorials, checklists, build logs.

**Core line:** The idea is not real until it has a link.

### 4. The Margins

Writing, grief, humor, spirituality, Jung, beauty, memory, longing, and the private emotional life behind public ambition.

**Best formats:** essays, poems, notes app style posts, Patreon exclusives.

**Core line:** The margin is where the real story usually lives.

## Weekly Publishing Rhythm

### Monday

Publish one authority post on LinkedIn. Use a carousel or teardown.

### Tuesday

Publish one TikTok or Reel. Use a direct-to-camera hook from the list.

### Wednesday

Publish one website, offer, or copywriting tip. Keep it practical.

### Thursday

Publish one personal essay excerpt or The Margins note.

### Friday

Publish one build log. Show the product, page, tool, or system you made.

### Weekend

Batch ideas. Do not doom scroll and call it market research.

## The Sales Path

Every piece of content should point to one of four places:

1. **Website audit** for people who need clearer positioning.
2. **Strategy session** for people who need help deciding what to build.
3. **Digital product** for people who want a lower cost starting point.
4. **The Margins** for people who want the writing and personal reinvention side.

Do not send every post to every offer. Match the post to the next step.
