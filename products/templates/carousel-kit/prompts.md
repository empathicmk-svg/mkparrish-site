# Carousel Prompts — Draft in Your Voice

Paste these into your AI tool of choice. Replace the brackets. Edit hard afterward —
the prompt gets you a draft, not a finished carousel. Your voice is the last 20%.

---

## 1. Find the one idea

> I'm a [role] who helps [audience] [outcome]. Give me 10 sharp "most people believe
> X, but the truth is Y" tensions from my field. Make them specific and a little
> contrarian — the kind of thing people in my space know but won't say out loud.
> No generic advice.

## 2. Build the teardown carousel

> Using this tension: "[paste your chosen tension]", draft a 7-slide LinkedIn carousel
> in the teardown format:
> 1) Cover — a strong promise with sharp tension
> 2) Problem — name what the audience gets wrong and what it costs them
> 3-5) Three before/after rewrites with a one-line takeaway each
> 6) Principle — the whole lesson in one screenshot-able rule
> 7) CTA — one specific next move
> Voice: clear over clever, specific outcomes not adjectives, one idea per slide,
> no em dashes, no manufactured inspiration, conversational and decisive.

## 3. Sharpen the cover

> Here's my cover line: "[paste]". Give me 8 alternatives that raise the tension
> without clickbait. Each must promise a specific outcome and hint at a cost. Short.

## 4. Cut to the rule

> Compress this lesson into one line short enough to screenshot: "[paste your principle]".
> Give me 5 versions, each under 12 words. No jargon.

## 5. De-slop pass

> Edit this carousel copy to remove every dead giveaway of AI writing: em dashes used
> for drama, "in today's fast-paced world," tricolons that say nothing, hedging, and
> empty adjectives. Make it sound like a sharp, decisive human wrote it fast and well.
> Keep my meaning. Here's the copy: [paste].
