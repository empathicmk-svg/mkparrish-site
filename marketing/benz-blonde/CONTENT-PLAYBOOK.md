# The Benz Blonde — Content & Growth Playbook

The microsite converts. This is what feeds it.

Everything below assumes one constraint: you are on the floor most days and you
do not have a production team. So nothing here takes more than fifteen minutes,
and most of it gets filmed between customers.

---

## 1. The positioning

**Nobody follows a car dealer. People follow a person who happens to sell cars.**

That sentence decides every content call you will ever make. The moment a video
sounds like the store made it, it dies. The moment it sounds like a friend who
works at the store and is telling you the truth, it travels.

Your unfair advantage is not the inventory. Every store has the inventory. Your
advantage is that you are willing to explain the parts of this business that stay
confusing *because confusion is profitable*. That is a genuinely uncommon
position, and it is defensible — a competitor cannot copy it without taking the
same risk you are taking.

**The through-line:** *I work at a dealership and I am still going to hand you
the list.*

---

## 2. The offer ladder

Content does not sell cars. Content earns the right to the next step. Every
piece should move someone exactly one rung.

| Rung | Ask | Where it lives |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Watch the video | IG / TikTok / YT |
| 1 | Follow | Profile |
| 2 | Get the 9 questions | Popup on the microsite |
| 3 | Send me your VIN / payoff | `/benz-blonde/trade` |
| 4 | Book a drive | `/benz-blonde/book` |
| 5 | Buy | The desk |
| 6 | Refer | `/benz-blonde/reviews` |

**The mistake to avoid:** jumping from rung 0 to rung 5. "Come see me at
Mercedes-Benz of Smithtown!" on a video from someone who has never heard of you
is a wasted CTA. Ask for rung 1 or 2. The site does the rest of the work.

## 3. The six formats

These are already on `/benz-blonde/watch` — this is the operator's version with
the production notes.

### Dealer Translation — weekly
One term per video. "Money factor." "Residual." "$500 over book."
**Structure:** the term (1s) → what they mean by it (10s) → what it costs you
(20s) → the sentence to say back (10s).
**Why it works:** it is genuinely useful to someone shopping *anywhere*, which is
why it gets shared into group chats. Shares are the only metric that compounds.

### Under Your Payment — weekly
"You said $700. Here are three."
**Structure:** the number → three cars → why each one gets there (lease vs.
finance vs. CPO) → "which one do you want the real figure on?"
**Production note:** film all three walkarounds in one lot trip. One trip, four
weeks of content.

### Delivery Day — every delivery
Thirty seconds. The bow, the keys, the face.
**Ask permission every single time, on camera or in writing.** No exceptions —
one person who feels used costs more than a hundred views are worth.
**Why it works:** nobody buys a car. People buy the feeling in that clip.

### Ask the Blonde — whenever the question is good
Reply-to-comment video. Screen-record the comment, answer it.
**Why it works:** highest trust-per-minute format that exists, and it costs
nothing. It also tells the algorithm your comments section is alive.
The site feeds this: `/benz-blonde/watch` has a form that collects questions.

### Equity Check — monthly
"2021 GLE owners: look at this."
**Structure:** specific model year → what it is actually worth right now → the
gap between that and what people assume → "send me your VIN."
**Why it works:** it is the only format with a *deadline built in*, because the
market moves. It is also your highest-intent lead source by a wide margin.

### The Walkaround — new arrivals
Not a spec read. **The one feature that sells the car, the one that disappoints
people, and who it is actually for.** Saying the disappointing part out loud is
the entire reason anyone believes the rest.

## 4. The first 30 days

Three posts a week is enough. Consistency beats volume, and a schedule you can
actually hold beats an ambitious one you abandon in week two.

**Week 1 — establish the premise**
1. *Dealer Translation:* "Money factor" — and why nobody at the store will convert it to an APR for you unless you ask.
2. *The Walkaround:* whatever just landed. Include the disappointing part.
3. *The origin story:* why you sell cars this way. One take, no script, shot in your car. This is the one that makes people follow rather than watch.

**Week 2 — prove usefulness**
4. *Under Your Payment:* pick the most common budget you hear on the floor.
5. *Dealer Translation:* "residual" — and why it silently decides your payment.
6. *Delivery Day:* first one you get permission for.

**Week 3 — create intent**
7. *Equity Check:* one specific model year that is worth more than owners think.
8. *Ask the Blonde:* answer the best comment from weeks 1–2.
9. *Lease-end timing:* six months vs. three months vs. thirty days. Point to `/benz-blonde/lease-end`.

**Week 4 — convert**
10. *The 9 questions:* film yourself reading three of them. "The other six are on my site." This is the single best popup driver you have.
11. *Under Your Payment* or a second *Walkaround*.
12. *Delivery Day* or a customer thanking you — with permission.

**The rule for month two:** look at which of the twelve got the most *shares and
saves*, not likes. Make four more of that one. Repeat forever.

## 5. Hooks that work in this category

The first 1.5 seconds is the whole job. Some openers that earn the next line:

- "You are about to overpay and you will not even know it."
- "Your car is worth more than you think. Here is how to check in thirty seconds."
- "A dealer told you X. Here is what they meant."
- "Do not sign that until you ask this one question."
- "I work at a dealership. I am about to cost myself money."
- "Three cars under [payment]. Number two surprises everyone."
- "The worst time to shop for a car is the last month of your lease. Here is why."
- "If your lease ends in six months, stop scrolling."

**Hooks that do not work:** "Hey guys, welcome back." "Check out this beautiful
GLE." "We have great deals this month." All three announce that you are an ad.

## 6. Bio and link strategy

Every profile bio should do three things in this order: **who you are, who it is
for, what to do next.**

> MK · The Benz Blonde 🤍
> Mercedes-Benz, Long Island. I tell you the real number first.
> The 9 questions dealers hope you skip ↓

Link straight to `mkparrish.com/benz-blonde`. Not a link tree — every extra tap
loses roughly half the people, and the microsite already *is* the link tree, with
a nav bar that does the same job and a signup popup a link tree cannot run.

**Platform-specific link notes:**
- TikTok/IG bio → `/benz-blonde` (popup fires at 50% scroll or 18s)
- A video specifically about lease end → `/benz-blonde/lease-end` in the comment
- A video about trade values → `/benz-blonde/trade`
- Stories → `/benz-blonde/book` directly; story viewers are already warm

## 7. Measuring it

Track exactly four numbers. Everything else is vanity.

1. **Leads per week** from the site (every form emails you with its source tag —
   `benz-trade`, `benz-book`, `popup:/benz-blonde`, and so on).
2. **Shares + saves per post.** Not likes. Shares mean it left the platform.
3. **Follower growth per week.** Slope, not total.
4. **Appointments set from social.** Ask every lead "where did you find me?" and
   write the answer down.

If leads are flat while followers grow, your content is entertaining and not
useful — shift toward Dealer Translation and Equity Check. If leads come but
followers are flat, you are converting the same small audience repeatedly — shift
toward shareable formats and hooks.

---

## 8. What not to do

- **Do not post inventory dumps.** A carousel of stock photos is a flyer. Nobody
  has ever followed a flyer.
- **Do not fake urgency.** "Only 2 left!" when there are eleven is the exact
  behavior your whole brand is positioned against. One catch and the premise is
  gone permanently.
- **Do not post a customer without permission**, and do not post a customer's
  numbers ever, permission or not.
- **Do not quote payments on camera** unless you say the disclosure out loud and
  it is still true when someone watches it four months later. Payments move. The
  site deliberately quotes none for this reason — every model card sends people
  to text you for today's number instead, which is also a better lead.
- **Do not argue in the comments.** Answer once, kindly, and let it go. The
  audience is watching how you handle it, not who is right.
