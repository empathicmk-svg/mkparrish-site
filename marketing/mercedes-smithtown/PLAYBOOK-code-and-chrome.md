# Code & Chrome: MK's niche and content playbook

The niche, the content pillars, a POV script bank, AI prompts, a stitch and
trend workflow, a sound plan and a 4-week calendar. Volume 7
(`build-vol7.mjs`, captions in `captions-vol7.md`) is the first set made from it.

## 1. The niche

**MK is the Mercedes insider who writes code.**

Plenty of people on CarTok sell cars, and plenty of creators code, write or
consult. Almost nobody does all four in public. Your overlap:

| You | What it gives the audience |
|---|---|
| Mercedes-Benz sales consultant | Access to the cars, the real numbers and the showroom |
| Coder | You *build* things: lease calculators, lead trackers, websites, data tools |
| Writer | Books, captions, stories, the words people repeat |
| Market consultant | Strategy: why an ad works, how a brand should grow |

**Positioning line:** *I sell Mercedes-Benz, and I show my math.*

Bio options:
- `Mercedes-Benz of Smithtown · I sell Benz, write code & books · DM "MATH" for real lease numbers`
- `Your Mercedes girl who codes · Long Island · test drives ↓`

Every post should answer yes to at least two of these:
1. Is it about Mercedes-Benz, the showroom, or buying a car?
2. Does it show something only someone who codes, writes or consults would show?
3. Would someone send it to a friend who's shopping, or to a woman in sales or tech?

## 2. Five content pillars

| Pillar | Format | Cadence | Goal |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Dealer Translation × code** | One car-buying term, explained with on-screen code: money factor, residual, cap cost, APR | Weekly | Shares, saves, DMs ("MATH") |
| **Trend-jacks** | Culture moments in the Mercedes world: F1 race weekends, holidays, memes, POV formats | 2× a week | Reach |
| **Showroom POV** | Walkarounds, new arrivals, delivery days (with permission), Friday vibes | 2× a week | Test drives |
| **Build in public** | The tools you code: calculators, lead trackers, your site. Show the build, not client data. | Weekly | Followers from tech, consulting leads |
| **Words & strategy** | Book lines, caption craft, "why this car ad works" teardowns | Weekly | Authority, consulting leads |

Guardrails:
- **No client data on screen**, ever. That includes Nature's Value numbers, customer names and real quotes, unless the customer says yes in writing.
- **No invented offers.** Only post an incentive, rate or sales event your manager has confirmed for this month.
- Example numbers say "example" on screen, as the lease reel does.

## 4. Prompt bank

Paste these into Claude or ChatGPT. Replace the `[brackets]`.

**Find sub-niches**
> I'm a Mercedes-Benz sales consultant on Long Island who also writes code, writes books and consults brands on marketing. List 15 specific content sub-niches where those skills overlap. For each one, give the audience, why they'd share it, and three video hooks under 8 words.

**Hooks**
> Write 20 on-screen hooks (max 7 words each) for a 15-second Reel about [topic, e.g. "money factor"]. Mix POV, "things nobody tells you", "wait for it" and question hooks. Tone: confident, warm, a little playful. No clickbait I can't back up.

**Dealer Translation script**
> Explain [term] for a car shopper in a 20-second script: the term (1 line), what it means (2 lines), what it costs them (1 line with an example labeled "example"), and the one sentence to say to a salesperson. Then write it as 6 lines of simple JavaScript that calculates it.

**Trend-jack**
> This week's trend is [trend or sound, with a link or description]. Write three ways a Mercedes-Benz salesperson who codes could use it without making fun of customers or the brand. Give the shots, the on-screen text, and where the beat drop should land.

**Stitch reply**
> Here's what a creator said in a video I want to stitch: "[quote]". Write a 15-second reply that adds something new from inside a Mercedes dealership. Agree or respectfully correct. Start with a 1-line hook.

**Captions**
> Write an Instagram caption (3 short lines plus a call to action to DM "[keyword]"), a TikTok caption (one line, lowercase, 6–8 hashtags), and a Facebook caption (2 sentences with my work and cell numbers) for a Reel about [topic]. Include #MercedesBenzOfSmithtown and #LongIsland.

**Repurpose for LinkedIn**
> Turn this Reel script into a 120-word LinkedIn post for sales and marketing professionals, with one takeaway about [selling/marketing/coding]: [script].

**Monthly review**
> Here are my last 30 posts with views, shares, saves and DMs: [paste]. Which pillar and hook style earned the most shares per view? What should I post more and less of next month?

## 5. Find it, stitch it: creators and content to react to

**Use only the app's own Stitch, Duet, Remix and Green Screen.** Those only work
when the creator allows them, and they credit the original. Never download
someone else's video and repost it, including brand videos.

`#carsalesman` · `"dealership secrets"` · `"lease explained"` ·

## 7. Sound plan

- **Every Volume 7 reel ships with its own scored soundtrack.** That's the
  118 BPM showroom track, plus a key click on every typed character, a chime on
  every printed line, and a whoosh and hit on every cut. It's all original, so
  it's cleared for Business accounts, which can't use most chart music.
- **To use a trending sound instead:** find it in TikTok's Creative Center
  (Trends → Songs, United States, last 7 days) or the Reels audio page, and look
  for rising sounds, the ones with the ↗ arrow. Add it in the app, turn the
  original audio to 0, and line its drop up with the first cut listed in
  `output/mercedes-smithtown/vol7/reels/beat-map.md`.
- On a **Business** account, choose from TikTok's Commercial Music Library and
  Instagram's licensed business audio only.

## 9. What to measure each week

1. **Shares per 1,000 views.** This is the number that compounds.
2. **Saves**, which signal useful content (Dealer Translation should win here).
3. **Keyword DMs**: "MATH", "MK" and VINs.
4. **Test-drive bookings** from `mkparrish.com/test-drive`.

Rule: if a format beats your average shares two weeks running, make two more
like it the next week. If it trails for three weeks, retire it.
