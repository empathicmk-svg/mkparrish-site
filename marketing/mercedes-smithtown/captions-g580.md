# "The G-Wagon went electric" (G 580)

A Reel/TikTok, a five-slide carousel, a feed post and two stories from your door-sill photo and two walkaround clips of the electric G 580. The files are in `output/mercedes-smithtown/g580/`. To rebuild them, run `node marketing/mercedes-smithtown/build-g580.mjs`.

The reel cuts on the beat over your showroom music bed, with a cool grade, a soft glow, a vignette and a chrome light sweep. It ends on **Call or text MK · 347.853.4238**, with the work line and the store address.

## Before posting

- **Specs and price.** The posts only claim what the photos show: the G 580 badge on the back, the rear-mounted spare, the boxy shape, the lit door sill and the round air vents. They don't give range, horsepower, charging time, model year or price. Check the window sticker before answering those in replies.
- **OK to post.** Get your manager's OK if the car is a unit someone has already put a deposit on. No plate number or VIN is visible.
- **No photos of you.** The instrument cluster reflects the person filming in some frames, so no cockpit video is used. The dash is shown as a still of the center stack only. Staff are in the background of the video from about 9.6s to 11.6s, so that stretch is left out.
- **Availability.** It's one car, so archive the posts once it sells.
- **The video's color.** Both clips were shot in HDR, so they were tone-mapped when they were converted.
- **The video's own sound.** It's only showroom noise, so the reel uses your music bed instead.

## When to post

- **Day 1:** the reel on Instagram and TikTok, plus story 01.
- **Day 2:** the carousel on Instagram and the same five slides as a TikTok photo post.
- **Day 3:** the feed post, plus story 02.

Post between 11am and 1pm or between 6pm and 8pm. If you add a trending sound in the app, turn the original audio down to 0. TikTok is @mk_parrish, the same as Instagram.
