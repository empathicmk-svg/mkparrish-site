# Trend reels: MK × Mercedes-Benz of Smithtown

Six Reels/TikToks cut from your own photos and clips, each built on a format
that's trending this fall. Files are in `output/mercedes-smithtown/viral/`. To
rebuild them, run `node marketing/mercedes-smithtown/build-viral.mjs`, then
`node scripts/add-music.mjs`.

## Adding the trending sound

Every video already has music: one of your original beds, cleared for any
account, so nothing posts silent. The cuts land on the beat, so a trending
song lines up too. The Instagram and TikTok publishing APIs can't attach a
song from their libraries, so the trending sound is added in the app:

1. In Metricool, schedule the post as **"Notify me"** (or post straight from
   the app at the time below).
2. **Instagram:** upload the video, tap **Audio**, search the song, then
   **Control → Original audio 0**. Drag the song so the chorus or drop starts
   at 0:00.
3. **TikTok:** upload, tap **Add sound**, search the song, then **Volume →
   Original sound 0**.

Trending sounds change weekly. Before posting, tap the sound and check for the
↗ arrow, which means it's still rising. Some big songs ("Patient Zero",
"Ramalama", "Nicole Kidman") are licensed, so **business accounts may not see
them**. If a song doesn't show up, use the backup sound or keep the music
already on the video.
