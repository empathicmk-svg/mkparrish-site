# "It parks itself": Active Parking Assist with PARKTRONIC

Files are in `output/mercedes-smithtown/parktronic/`. To rebuild them, run
`node marketing/mercedes-smithtown/build-parktronic.mjs`. The Instagram,
TikTok and Facebook captions are in `POSTS['reel-parks-itself']` in
`schedule.mjs`.

## The plan

The hook is curiosity: a steering wheel turning with nobody touching it.
People watch to the end to see whether the car makes it into the space, and
that watch-through is what Instagram and TikTok reward. Every piece asks the
same question, *"Does your car park itself?"*, so people answer in comments
and poll taps. Those answers push the reel to more people.

| When (New York) | Where | What |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fri Oct 16, 9:30 | Instagram story | Poll story (`stories/01-poll-park-itself.jpg`). Metricool sends you a reminder. Add the poll sticker: **"Does your car park itself?"** with the answers **YES 😎** and **I WISH 😩**. |
| Fri Oct 16, 10:00 | Instagram Reel + TikTok | `reels/01-it-parks-itself.mp4`. Friday at 10am is your strongest Instagram slot in Metricool, and near the top for TikTok. |
| Fri Oct 16, right after it posts | Instagram story | Share the reel to your story with a "watch the wheel 👀" text. |
| Fri Oct 16, first hour | Comments | Reply to every comment. Answer "I WISH" with "Come try it, DM me PARK 😉". |
| Sat Oct 17 | Instagram story | Share the poll results ("72% said I WISH… I can fix that 😉") with a link sticker to `mkparrish.com/test-drive`. |

The 9:30 poll warms your audience up, so the reel lands with people already
engaged.

## Before posting

- **Feature name.** Mercedes calls it *Active Parking Assist with
  PARKTRONIC*. PARKTRONIC is the sensor system. The captions and the reel keep
  the line "Driver must stay attentive and ready to take over", because the
  driver is still responsible.
- **Model.** The car isn't named, because the clip doesn't show which model
  it is. If someone asks, check the window sticker before answering.
- **Sound.** The reel keeps the clip's own sound: the parking chimes make it
  feel real. On Instagram you can add a trending song quietly underneath.
- **Facebook.** Facebook isn't connected in Metricool, so the Facebook post
  isn't scheduled. Copy the `fb` caption from `schedule.mjs` and post the reel
  by hand, or connect your Facebook page in Metricool (Brand settings →
  Connections) and it can be scheduled.
