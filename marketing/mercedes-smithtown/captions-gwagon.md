# "2027 G-Wagon, today"

A Reel/TikTok, a story and two feed posts from MK's walkaround of the matte 2027 G-Class on the showroom floor. The files are in `output/mercedes-smithtown/gwagon/`. To rebuild them, run `node marketing/mercedes-smithtown/build-gwagon.mjs`, then `node scripts/add-music.mjs`.

The reel cuts on the beat with zoom, wipe and flash transitions. It has a cool showroom grade, a soft glow, a vignette and a chrome light sweep. Everything ends on **Call or text MK · 347.853.4238**, with your work line too.

## Before posting

- **Model and trim.** Check the window sticker before naming the exact model (G 550 or AMG G 63) in a reply. The posts only say "2027 G-Class" and "G-Wagon".
- **Availability.** It's a one-off car, so post the reel and story the same day, and delete or archive them once it sells.
- **Price.** No price is in the posts. When someone asks, answer in DMs.

## When to post

Post the reel and the story the same day, ideally between 11am and 1pm or between 6pm and 8pm. Put a link sticker to `mkparrish.com/test-drive` on the story, or a "Call" sticker. Post feed 01 the same day and feed 02 two days later, if the car is still there.

Suggested sound: a hard-hitting trending sound such as "Money Trees", Kendrick Lamar, or any trending "luxury car" sound. The video already has your showroom music bed. If you add a song in the app, turn the original audio down to 0.
