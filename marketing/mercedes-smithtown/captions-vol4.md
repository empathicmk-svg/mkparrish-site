# Volume 4: "Walk in with me"

Five Reels/TikToks and four feed posts from MK's latest photos and clips. The
files are in `output/mercedes-smithtown/vol4/`. To rebuild them, run
`node marketing/mercedes-smithtown/build-vol4.mjs`, then
`node scripts/add-music.mjs`.

Every reel already has the "showroom" music bed, and its cuts land on the
beat. To use a trending song instead, add it in the app and turn the original
audio down to 0. The steps are in `captions-viral.md`.

## Before posting

- **Delivery photo (`feed/02-delivered`).** The customers' faces are in it.
  Their plate and the names on the sign are blurred, but text them first and
  get a yes before you post.
- **GLE walkaround.** Check the window sticker for the exact model and trim in
  case someone asks.
- **Member savings.** Only promise what the program offers. The reel and post
  say "you may qualify" and "see if you qualify" on purpose.

## Posting plan

The 8pm slots stay clear of the posts already in the calendar and of the
trend reels in `captions-viral.md`.
