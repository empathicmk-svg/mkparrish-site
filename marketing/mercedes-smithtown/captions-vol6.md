# Volume 6: "Book it"

Volume 6 is three Reels/TikToks and one story. Every piece ends on **mkparrish.com/test-drive**. The files are in `output/mercedes-smithtown/vol6/`. To rebuild them, run `node marketing/mercedes-smithtown/build-vol6.mjs`, then `node scripts/add-music.mjs`.

## Strategy

Every post points people to one place: **mkparrish.com/test-drive**. That page books a test drive, a fleet or Sprinter quote, or a custom factory order. People who book there become leads with a name, phone number, car and time already filled in.

- **Hooks that stop the scroll:** "Green flags at a car dealership", "How to test drive a Mercedes this week" and "Things nobody tells you" are formats people share and save. Saves and shares push a reel to non-followers more than likes do.
- **Two ways to respond:** each caption gives a DM keyword (BOOK, BUILD or GREEN) and the website link. Anyone who DMs the keyword gets the link from you. That reply also counts as engagement, so the reel gets shown to more people.
- **Your link in bio:** set your Instagram and TikTok bio link to `mkparrish.com/test-drive` while these run. Then "link in bio" lands people straight on the booking form.
- **Story:** post the story the same night as each reel. Put a link sticker to `mkparrish.com/test-drive` in the open space above the headline.
- **Pin it:** pin "Book it in 30 seconds" to the top of your profile.
