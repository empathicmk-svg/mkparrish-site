# Volume 7: "Code & Chrome"

Four Reels/TikToks and two feed posts for MK's niche: the Mercedes consultant
who also writes code, writes books and consults brands. The strategy behind
them is in `PLAYBOOK-code-and-chrome.md`. The files are in
`output/mercedes-smithtown/vol7/`. To rebuild, run
`node marketing/mercedes-smithtown/build-vol7.mjs`. It renders the code
animations and scores each reel's soundtrack itself, so `add-music` isn't needed.

**Code scenes:** every code screen is real JavaScript, typed out on screen in
your brand colors. The lease math is correct for the example shown:
$60,000 MSRP, 58% residual, 36 months and a 0.0025 money factor give $700 of
depreciation plus $237 of rent charge, which is $937 a month before tax and
fees, at an APR of about 6.0%.

**Sound:** each reel has its own soundtrack, timed to its cuts: a key click on
every typed character, a chime on each printed line, and a hit on every cut,
over your 118 BPM showroom track. It's all original, so it's safe on Business
accounts. To use a trending sound instead, see
`vol7/reels/beat-map.md` for where the first cut lands.
