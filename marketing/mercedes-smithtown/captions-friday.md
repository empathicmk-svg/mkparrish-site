# Friday reels

Two Reels/TikToks for Friday nights. To rebuild them, run `node marketing/mercedes-smithtown/build-friday.mjs`, then `node scripts/add-music.mjs`. Both get the same finish: scenes change on the beat with varied transitions, then a cool night grade, a soft glow, a vignette and drifting pink sparkles. Both end on **Book a weekend test drive · mkparrish.com/test-drive**.

Post one each Friday, between 6pm and 8pm New York time. Share it to your story right after it goes up, with a link sticker to `mkparrish.com/test-drive`.

## Reel 1 · Friday night vibes (`friday/friday-night-vibes.mp4`)

Cut from MK's showroom pan (the white AMG GT to her desk), the pink roses on her desk, her "Stay positive. Work hard. Make it happen." desk mat and a bow-collar selfie. It ends on "Friday night plans?"

Suggested sound: "Espresso", Sabrina Carpenter. A backup is any trending "Friday night" or "weekend mode" sound. The video already has your showroom music bed. If you add a song in the app, turn the original audio down to 0.
