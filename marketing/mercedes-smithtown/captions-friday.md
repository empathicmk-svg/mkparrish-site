One Reel/TikTok cut from MK's showroom pan (the white AMG GT to her desk), the pink roses on her desk, her "Stay positive. Work hard. Make it happen." desk mat and a bow-collar selfie. The file is `output/mercedes-smithtown/friday/friday-night-vibes.mp4`. To rebuild it, run `node marketing/mercedes-smithtown/build-friday.mjs`, then `node scripts/add-music.mjs`.
