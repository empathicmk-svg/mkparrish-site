# Sprinter delivery: Beatrice & Vertieres Legacy LLC

A Reel/TikTok, two stories and a three-slide feed carousel for the Sprinter
delivery. They're built from the welcome sign, the cockpit, an HD still of the
seat rows and HD stills and a clip from the earlier Sprinter walkaround. **There are no photos of
MK.** The files are in `output/mercedes-smithtown/delivery-sprinter/`. To
rebuild, run `node marketing/mercedes-smithtown/build-delivery-sprinter.mjs`.
It scores the reel's soundtrack itself: the showroom track with a hit on every
cut, at 2.03s, 4.58s, 7.12s, 9.15s and 11.19s.

## Before posting

- **Ask Beatrice first.** The posts use her first name and the company name,
  and the sign is in the photo. Text her the carousel, and ask whether she'd
  like to be tagged and what her or Vertieres Legacy's handle is. If she'd rather
  stay private, post story 02 and carousel slides 2–3 only, and use the
  "no names" captions at the end of this file.
- **Seat count.** The posts say "15-passenger", matching the earlier Sprinter
  set. If this van is set up differently, change `MODEL` in the build script
  and rerun it.
- **Business delivery:** this one belongs on LinkedIn too. There's a caption below.

## When to post

The same day, or the day after, the delivery: the reel and story 01 at 11 AM to
1 PM, and the carousel that evening. Add story 02 a few hours after story 01,
with a link sticker to `mkparrish.com/test-drive` with the text **YOUR TURN**.

Suggested sound: keep the scored soundtrack. If you swap in a trending sound,
pick something warm and celebratory, and line its drop up with the first cut at 2.03s.
