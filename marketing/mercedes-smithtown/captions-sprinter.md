# "Sprinter 15, customized"

A Reel/TikTok, two stories and five feed posts. They mix MK's walkaround of the black 15-passenger Sprinter on the lot with custom upfit interiors from High Quality Custom Design. The walkaround footage is stabilized. The upfit photos were straightened from screen photos, cleaned up and shown full size, one per frame. The files are in `output/mercedes-smithtown/sprinter/`. To rebuild them, run `node marketing/mercedes-smithtown/build-sprinter.mjs`, then `node scripts/add-music.mjs`.

The reel cuts on the beat with zoom, wipe and flash transitions. It has a cool grade, a soft glow, a vignette and a chrome light sweep. Everything ends on **Call or text MK · 347.853.4238**, with your work line too.

## Before posting

- **Seats and upfit.** Check the window sticker and the upfitter sheet before naming specific features in replies, such as the roof height, the wheelbase, the seat count or what was customized. The posts only say "15-passenger" and "customized".
- **Availability.** It's a one-off van, so post the reel and story the same day, and archive them once it sells.
- **Upfit photos.** The custom interiors are High Quality Custom Design's photos of their Ambassador Sprinter builds and of the store's latest upfit. Every post credits "Upfits by High Quality Custom Design". Check with your manager that you can use their photos, and tag @hqcustomdesign if they have an account. The van on the lot is the 15-passenger. The custom interiors show what a Sprinter *can* be built into, so don't say the van on the lot has them.
- **Price and fleet pricing.** No price is in the posts. When someone asks, answer in DMs. Business buyers may ask about fleet or commercial incentives.

## When to post

Post the reel and the story on a weekday morning, between 7am and 9am or between 11am and 1pm, when business owners are scrolling. Put a link sticker to `mkparrish.com/test-drive` on the story, or a "Call" sticker. Post feed 01 the same day and feed 02 two days later, if the van is still there.

Suggested sound: a confident, upbeat trending sound such as "Started From the Bottom", Drake, or any trending "new work truck" or "fleet day" sound. The video already has your showroom music bed. If you add a song in the app, turn the original audio down to 0.
