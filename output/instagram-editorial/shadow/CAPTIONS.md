## 01. As Fate (Sat Nov 14)

> when an inner situation is not made conscious, it happens outside, as fate. — carl jung · aion, 1951

```
this is the jung line everyone shares as "until you make the unconscious conscious, it will direct your life and you will call it fate." he never wrote that sentence. this is what he actually wrote, in aion, 1951. and honestly? the real one is sharper. the pattern you refuse to look at doesn't disappear. it starts showing up as other people, other jobs, other relationships. same lesson, new outfit. look at it on purpose and it stops having to find you. ♡ - MK

## 04. Within Our Power (Fri Nov 20)

> there are things which are within our power, and there are things which are beyond our power. — epictetus · enchiridion

```
epictetus was born enslaved and became one of the great teachers of stoicism, and this is how his handbook opens. sort everything into two piles. in your power: your effort, your words, your next move. not in your power: their reply, the timeline, the outcome. spend your energy on the first pile. detachment is just good sorting. ♡ - MK

## 08. Everyone Carries a Shadow (Sat Nov 28)
