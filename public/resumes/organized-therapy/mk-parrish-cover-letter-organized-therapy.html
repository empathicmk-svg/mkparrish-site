Dear Victoria,

Your clients say your team helped them unpack in record time and gave them ideas they'd never have thought of. I'd love to help deliver that feeling, one closet, garage and move at a time.

I'm writing to ask about joining Organized Therapy as a Professional Organizer. I'll be upfront: I haven't organized homes professionally. What I've done for nearly two decades is build systems people can actually keep up with, and take care of clients who are trusting me with something important.

At Ericsson I built the playbooks and organized libraries dozens of partners relied on. At DEFY Media I kept 150+ client accounts current at once. At Mercer I ran 50+ executive events down to every detail. Today I guide clients through a big, personal purchase at Mercedes-Benz of Smithtown, where patience and discretion matter as much as they will in someone's home.

What I'd bring to your team:

  • In the home. Patience, discretion and the energy to sort, lift, pack and unpack, with simple systems clients can keep up.
  • On the details. Schedules, supplies and follow-ups handled, so every session starts ready and ends on time.
  • Beyond the job. I designed and built my own website, mkparrish.com, and I create short videos and posts. I'd be glad to help share your before-and-afters and grow your bookings.

I live on Long Island and I'm flexible on schedule. I'd welcome the chance to meet, or to shadow a session and show you how I work.

Thank you,

Mary Kate Parrish
