Make Amazon your strongest channel.

Dear Nature's Value Hiring Team,

Your products already sell. At other retailers they return about $20 for every dollar invested. On Amazon, the return is closer to $0.15. That gap is the opportunity I'd like to own.

I'm applying for your marketing role because I've spent nearly two decades turning trusted brands into products people buy, across retail, partner, and consumer channels. Most recently I ran national marketing for Atlantic Ultraviolet, a Long Island manufacturer 20 minutes from Coram, across its distributor, dealer and direct-to-consumer channels. I know how a manufacturer's margins, timelines and sales teams work, and I know marketing only counts when it shows up in sell-through.

I'd treat Amazon the way a CFO would and run it the way a marketer should:

  • Stop the bleed. Build a P&L for every ASIN, cut ad spend that can't break even, and win back the Buy Box from unauthorized sellers.
  • Rebuild to convert. Better listings, A+ content and Dr. Michael Murray on video, plus bundles that lift order value above fee thresholds.
  • Scale with gates. Amazon gets the largest budget, but each dollar is released only when a product proves it pays for itself, with Subscribe & Save turning first orders into repeat revenue.

My 90-day target is to triple Amazon ROI, then grow it into a channel that earns more than it costs. The track record behind that: $200M+ in partner-influenced revenue at Ericsson, $20M+ in retail product revenue at Take-Two, and a creator network I grew to 80M+ monthly viewers at DEFY Media, where I learned how one trusted face can become a channel people come back to.

I put the full plan in writing, including the budget split, unit math and weekly KPIs, at mkparrish.com/natures-value. I'd welcome the chance to walk you through it.

Warmly,
