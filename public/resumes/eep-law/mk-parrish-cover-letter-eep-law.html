Dear Hiring Manager,

When someone is hurt, the two things they need from a law firm's staff are simple: somebody who calls back, and somebody who gets every detail right. I'd like to be that person at Elefterakis, Elefterakis & Panek.

I'm applying for a Paralegal position. I'm changing careers, and I want to say that plainly: I haven't worked in a law office. What I bring is nearly two decades of the work a paralegal's day is made of, managing clients, documents and deadlines, and I'm ready to learn the legal side properly.

At DEFY Media I kept 150+ client accounts current at once. At Mercer I negotiated global vendor contracts and ran 50+ executive networks, down to the agendas and logistics. At Ericsson I built the document libraries dozens of partners relied on, and ran reviews that tracked every open item to its next step. I'm also a published author and ghostwriter, so drafting, editing and proofreading are what I do every day.

What I'd take off your attorneys' desks:

  • Client contact. Same-day callbacks and clear updates, so clients feel heard and attorneys aren't interrupted.
  • Files and records. Requesting, tracking and organizing documents so every file is complete.
  • Drafts and deadlines. Clean first drafts for attorney review, a careful second read, and a calendar nothing falls off.

Your firm's work for injured people, including construction workers, matters to me. I work with Long Island builders and trade contractors through the Long Island Builders Institute, so the people your construction cases protect are people I know.

I'd welcome the chance to meet, and I'm glad to complete a writing or proofreading test.

Thank you,

Mary Kate Parrish
