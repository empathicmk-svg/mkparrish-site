# Carousel: The Copy Audit Checklist

## Slide 1
Before you redesign the page, audit the copy.

## Slide 2
Clarity: can a stranger explain it?

## Slide 3
Specificity: could a competitor say the same sentence?

## Slide 4
Proof: where does the claim become believable?

## Slide 5
Friction: what objection is still unhandled?

## Slide 6
Action: what should the reader do next?

## Slide 7
CTA: Run the Website Leak Map.
