# Carousel: The 7 Sentences Every Homepage Needs

## Slide 1
Your homepage does not need more copy. It needs the right seven sentences.

## Slide 2
Who this is for: name the buyer before you describe yourself.

## Slide 3
What changed: show the problem that made the old way stop working.

## Slide 4
What you do: use a verb, not a category.

## Slide 5
Why it matters: connect the work to a business consequence.

## Slide 6
Proof it works: add numbers, examples, before/after, or named experience.

## Slide 7
What happens next: make the next step obvious and low-friction.

CTA: Run the Website Leak Map.
