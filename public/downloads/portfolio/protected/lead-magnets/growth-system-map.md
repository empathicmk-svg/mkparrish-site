# Growth System Map

Use this when marketing activity is high but pipeline movement is unclear.

## Map The Motion

1. Audience  
Who is the exact account, role, or buyer moment?

2. Offer  
What are they being asked to believe or do?

3. Asset  
What page, video, carousel, audit, or proof point moves them forward?

4. Channel  
Where does the buyer first encounter it?

5. Handoff  
What happens after they click, reply, download, or book?

6. Measurement  
What metric tells us the system is working?

## Warning Signs

- Campaigns exist but the offer is unclear.
- Content gets attention but not qualified conversations.
- Sales has no strong follow-up asset.
- Paid traffic lands on pages with weak proof.
- Every channel has a different message.

## Fix

Build the system around one buyer decision at a time.
