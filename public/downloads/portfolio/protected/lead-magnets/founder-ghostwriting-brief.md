# Founder Ghostwriting Brief

Use this before writing for a founder or executive.

## Voice Capture

1. What phrases does the founder naturally use?
2. What phrases would they never say?
3. What topics make them sharper or more alive?
4. What belief do they hold that their industry softens?
5. What stories do they repeat in sales calls?
