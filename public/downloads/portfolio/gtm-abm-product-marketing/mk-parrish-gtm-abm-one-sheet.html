GTM and ABM One-Sheet

A one-page read on how MK thinks about pipeline, account motion, and copy that survives the handoff to sales.

GTM ABM Product Marketing Bebas Neue

GTM operating thesis

Most GTM problems show up as channel problems, but start as language problems. The ICP is too broad. The trigger is soft. The offer is described from the company side. The proof is not placed where fear appears. The next step asks too much or too little.

The fix is a message system that can travel from the first touch to the sales call without losing specificity.

ABM translation

  • Target account: name the business pressure, not just the industry.
  • Buying committee: write for the CFO, operator, champion, and skeptic separately.
  • Proof placement: map each claim to the objection it resolves.
  • Sales handoff: give reps the sentence, asset, and follow-up path after the click.
  • Measurement: track qualified replies, page engagement, booked calls, and proof consumption.

What I would build

  • Account-room narrative: one problem, one market shift, one proof path.
  • Landing page message map: hero, stakes, proof, objection, CTA.
  • Outbound sequence: trigger-led email and LinkedIn follow-up with a specific asset.
  • Sales enablement: objection snippets, proof bank, and follow-up notes.
