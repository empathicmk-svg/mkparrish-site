The quotes I return to when the writing won't come, or won't get clean. On craft, on cutting, on the strange discipline of saying a true thing so plainly that it lands. If one of these gets you back to the page, it did its job.

---

> The secret of good writing is to strip every sentence to its cleanest components.
> **— William Zinsser**, *On Writing Well* · Clarity

> Omit needless words.
> **— Strunk & White**, *The Elements of Style* · Editing

> Perfection is achieved not when there is nothing more to add, but when there is nothing left to take away.
> **— Antoine de Saint-Exupéry** · Subtraction

> The difference between the almost right word and the right word is the difference between the lightning bug and the lightning.
> **— Mark Twain** · Word Choice

> I have made this letter longer than usual, only because I have not had the time to make it shorter.
> **— Blaise Pascal** · Brevity

> Try to leave out the part that readers tend to skip.
> **— Elmore Leonard** · Editing

> Don't tell me the moon is shining; show me the glint of light on broken glass.
> **— Anton Chekhov** · Show, Don't Tell

> Kill your darlings, kill your darlings, even when it breaks your egocentric little scribbler's heart.
> **— Stephen King**, *On Writing* · The Cut

> The first draft of anything is the hardest to forgive and the most important to finish.
> **— after Ernest Hemingway** · Drafts

> Almost all good writing begins with terrible first efforts. You need to start somewhere.
> **— Anne Lamott**, *Bird by Bird* · Drafts

> You don't start out writing good stuff. You start out writing crap and thinking it's good stuff, and then gradually you get better at it.
> **— Octavia E. Butler** · Practice

> Write hard and clear about what hurts.
> **— Ernest Hemingway** · Truth

> Good prose is like a windowpane.
> **— George Orwell**, *Why I Write* · Clarity

> The consumer isn't a moron; she is your wife.
> **— David Ogilvy**, *Confessions of an Advertising Man* · Respect for the Reader

> If it doesn't sell, it isn't creative.
> **— David Ogilvy** · Persuasion

> We are drowning in information but starved for knowledge.
> **— John Naisbitt**, *Megatrends* · Attention

> The job of the writer is to make revolution irresistible.
> **— Toni Cade Bambara** · Purpose

> Easy reading is damn hard writing.
> **— Nathaniel Hawthorne** · Craft
