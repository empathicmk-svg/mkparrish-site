title: Drafts

They tell you to wait till the writing is clean,
till the life is presentable, edited, seen —
but the page that I love is the one with the scars,
the margins still crowded, the crossed-out, the ours.

The first draft is honest in ways the last hides.
It keeps all the doubting, the second-guess sides,
the arrow that says *move this, this came too soon,*
the ink that bled through from a different afternoon.

I used to believe I'd be ready *one day* —
when the mess was resolved and the fear cleared away.
But the mess is the method. The fear is the proof
that I'm writing toward something too true to be smooth.

So I keep the rough pages. I date them. I stay.
I let the bad sentences light the right way.
Because nothing arrives in the world fully made —
not a brand, not a book, not a life, not a grade.

It comes as a draft. As a guess. As a try.
As a line you'd be almost embarrassed to write,
then return to at midnight and finally see
was the truest thing there on the whole rough debris.

The polished comes later. The polished is fine.
But it can't say a thing that the draft didn't find.
So here, in the margins, the crossing-out part —
this is the room where I keep my real heart.

— *Drafts*, MK Parrish
