## Publishing notes

- **Free posts** seed and grow the list; they do the selling for the paid tier.
- **Paid posts** (12, 25–29) are the frameworks/teardowns/templates a subscriber pays for. Each opens with a free preview, then `/paywall`.
- **Poems** (30, 31) are emotional anchors — good pinned/welcome material, high share rate.
- **Collections** (32, 33) are low-effort, shareable, list-growth pieces.
- **34** is a cornerstone "what I do" piece — pin it; it converts readers into clients, not just subscribers.

CTAs rotate across: the strategy call (`mkparrish.com/book`), the digital products (Write Yourself Into the Room, Brand Voice Playbook, The Vault), and subscribing/upgrading on Substack — so no single post feels like the same ask twice.
