## Paste-Ready Post

I read a founder's birth chart before I read her pitch deck.

Half joking. Only half.

Not because Mercury closes deals — because after a decade of doing this, I've learned the fastest way to honest positioning isn't another brand questionnaire. It's anything that gets someone to stop performing and say the true thing about how they're wired.

Which brings me to "manifesting."

Every quarter, a founder tells me they want to manifest a bigger year. And every quarter, when it works, it's never because the universe kept a ledger. It's because setting the intention out loud forced them to do three deeply unmystical things:

1. Decide on ONE specific outcome — not "more clients," but this client, this offer, this number.

2. Say it in language a buyer can actually repeat. (Your happiest customer can't refer you if she can't explain what you do at dinner.)

3. Show up on that message long enough that the market believes it before they do.

That's it. That's the spell.

You don't attract what you want. You attract what you can describe.

So here's the drill I give founders who want the breakthrough-year energy without the wishful thinking:

→ Say your number out loud. The real one, not the safe one.
→ If you lowered your voice, you just found the actual constraint — and it isn't the market.
→ A goal you're embarrassed by is a goal you'll quietly sabotage.

Manifesting for business starts the moment you can name the number without flinching, then reverse-engineer the three moves that get you there and repeat them past the point of boredom.

Vague isn't humble. Vague is expensive.

The most spiritual thing you can do for your business this quarter is get specific.
